Welcome to Campus Coding Club

The Campus Coding Club is a student-led community focused on collaborative learning, project building, and mastering modern software development skills. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced programmer, there is a place for you here!

What We Do

  • Weekly Hack Nights: Hands-on coding sessions and collaborative project work.
  • Guest Workshops: Interactive talks by industry professionals on Web Development, AI, and Cloud.
  • Competitive Programming: Algorithmic problem-solving sessions and hackathons.
  • Open-Source Mentorship: Learn how to contribute to real-world open-source repositories on GitHub.
