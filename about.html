Our Mission & Vision

Founded in 2022, our mission is to foster an inclusive environment where students from all academic disciplines can develop core programming skills, build portfolio-ready web projects, and connect with peer mentors.

Why Join Us?

  1. Gain hands-on coding experience outside the traditional classroom setup.
